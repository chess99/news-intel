**链接**: https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/emo

**原文**: "EMO lets you use a small subset of its experts - just 12.5% of the total - for a given task while keeping near full-model performance, and still works as a strong general-purpose model when all experts are used together."

**🔗 历史关联**: 无直接关联知识库事件；该研究属于基础模型架构层面的学术贡献。

**⚠️ 缺失信息**: 原文未提供具体benchmark数值（"near full-model performance"未量化）、未披露训练算力成本、未说明与同类MoE（如Mixtral）推理效率的实际对比、未明确模型开源许可细节。
