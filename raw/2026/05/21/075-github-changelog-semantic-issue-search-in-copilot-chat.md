What&rsquo;s new Instead of relying only on exact matches and manual filters, Copilot Chat can now understand the intent of your query and surface issues that are semantically related even when they are worded differently. You can use this capability to help you find an issue in a repository using natural language when you don&rsquo;t remember the exact title or keywords, or to quickly filter issues related to a specific platform or environment.
