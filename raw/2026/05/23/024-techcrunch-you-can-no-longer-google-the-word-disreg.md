# You can no longer Google the word ‘disregard’

**来源**: TechCrunch | **发布**: 2026-05-23 | **链接**: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/22/you-can-no-longer-google-the-word-disregard/

## RSS 摘要

After Google Search's AI update, the word "disregard" now effectively breaks the search interface.

## 正文

In Brief Posted: 9:16 AM PDT · May 22, 2026 Image Credits: Google CEO Sundar Pichai at the Google I/O 2024 event Russell Brandom You can no longer Google the word &#8216;disregard&#8217; Earlier this week, Google rolled out a completely new Search experience , foregrounding AI summaries and kicking the traditional &#8220;ten blue links&#8221; far down the page. But the sheer scale of Google Search means there are lots of edge cases that the company doesn&#8217;t seem to have considered. For instance, this is what you&#8217;ll now get if you type the word &#8220;disregard&#8221; into Google Search. Screenshot Image Credits: Screenshot Google has been catching some flack on social media for this, and it&#8217;s easy to see why. As you&#8217;ll notice, the Merriam-Webster link is still in there, but you have to scroll past a huge block of empty space. For most users, that single reply is the only thing you&#8217;ll see. And crucially, the AI response serves no conceivable value to a user searching the word &#8220;disregard.&#8221; It&#8217;s just a broken tool. For context, here is the same search in Bing, which has been less aggressive about its AI summaries. It&#8217;s not perfect, but there is some useful information to be found here.
