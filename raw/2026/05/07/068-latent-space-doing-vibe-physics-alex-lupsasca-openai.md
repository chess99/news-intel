What was interesting is not only that ChatGPT solved this problem, but how it solved it. The model quickly realized found a limiting case (known as the “half-collinear regime”), that in hindsight has a nice intuitive explanation 4 . Taking this limit, the gnarly results collapsed down to a simple and intuitive formula! The last step was to prove this intuitive formula. The team started with a fresh session, gave a prompt with the context of what they previously learned, and let the model loose. Not only was ChatGPT able to reproduce the previous result, it was able to prove it using a technique unknown to the authors! The Vibe Physics moment With a concrete success in the bag, the team asked if they could generate new physics from scratch using ChatGPT.
