Bio Adam Wolff currently works at Anthropic as an engineer and individual contributor to Claude Code, the first terminal-based agentic coding tool. Previously, he was the Head of Engineering at Robinhood, and before that, he was in charge of Facebook's product infrastructure group, which was responsible for popular open source technologies including React and GraphQL.
